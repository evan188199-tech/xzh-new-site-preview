Who confirms the quote?

Sales / commercial owners. AI may help draft or organize notes later; it must not auto-commit price or lead time on this site.

Is live chat on?

No. Chatwoot is installed for later local testing only and is intentionally not embedded here.

Is this the official website?

No. Production remains at xzh-furniture.com until leadership chooses a public host for this new site.
