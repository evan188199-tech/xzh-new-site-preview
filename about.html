About XZH

Foshan Xuzhihui Household Products Co., Ltd. (XZH Furniture / 旭智汇家居) serves custom and project furniture needs. Public brand materials date the name to around 2010. We work with designers, developers, and buyers who need furniture that can move from brief to production and delivery.

This site is the company entry for international B2B inquiries: capabilities overview, FAQ, and a structured inquiry form so sales can review fit before quoting.

How we work

  • Facts over slogans — we do not invent growth metrics or ranking promises.
  • Human ownership of price, contract, and delivery commitments.
  • Case studies and named clients only after rights and accuracy are confirmed.
  • Concept renders and as-built photos are kept distinct when we show visuals.

For detailed address and phone, use the inquiry form or ask sales — we publish contact fields when operations confirms them.
