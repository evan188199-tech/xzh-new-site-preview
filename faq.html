FAQ

Practical answers for project buyers and designers. Commercial details are always confirmed by sales for your drawings and destination.

What should I include in a first inquiry?

Your name, a reachable email or WhatsApp, rough project location, furniture need (custom / standard / package), whether sizes or drawings exist, quantity hints, and preferred language. Photos or floor plans help; they are not a hard gate for first contact.

Custom vs standard — which path fits?

Choose custom when dimensions, joinery, or finishes must follow site drawings. Choose standard when you need ready-to-specify collections for living, dining, bedroom, home office, or kids spaces. Many projects mix both — tell us the room list and we advise.

Do you have MOQ or samples?

MOQ depends on product type, finish run, and whether the order is custom or standard. Sample pieces or finish boards can often be arranged after we understand the brief. Exact MOQ and sample cost are confirmed by sales — we do not publish a fixed number here.

How do drawings and site measure work?

Send whatever you have: CAD, PDF, sketches, or photos with dimensions. For custom cabinets and wardrobes, production typically needs final site measures or approved shop drawings. If you are overseas, we coordinate remote review first, then align on who measures on site.

What about materials, finishes, and fire ratings?

We discuss substrates, veneers, laminates, paints, hardware, and performance needs against your destination rules. Fire, formaldehyde, or other compliance requirements should be stated in the inquiry. We do not list certificate numbers on this page; sales shares relevant options after reviewing the brief.

How long is lead time?

Lead time depends on scope, finishes, factory load, and shipping method. Ranges are confirmed after drawings (or clear specs) and destination are agreed — not as a fixed week count on the website.

Shipping, FOB, and destination markets?

We support project shipments and discuss Incoterms (for example FOB or other terms) case by case. Destination country, port, packing, and installation responsibility must be clear before a commercial offer. We do not claim a locked list of markets on this site.

How does quoting work?

1) You send an inquiry. 2) Sales checks fit and may ask for missing sizes or finish preferences. 3) A human-owned quote is prepared — never an automatic price from this form. 4) Revisions follow drawing updates until both sides agree.

Who confirms the quote?

Sales / commercial owners. Tools may help organize notes later; they must not auto-commit price or lead time on this site.

What about payment terms?

Payment schedule and method are set in the sales contract for each order. We do not publish fixed deposit percentages on this page — ask sales for terms that match your project size and shipping plan.

After-sales and warranty?

We support project after-sales through the sales contact for the order. Warranty scope (parts, finish, workmanship) is written into the contract and depends on product type and destination. Contact us with the order reference if an issue arises after delivery.

Which languages and channels do you use?

This site is bilingual Chinese / English. Inquiries via the contact form or email / WhatsApp are preferred. Live website chat is not enabled on this page; our team replies through the channel you provide.

Do you work with designers and project partners?

Yes. Interior designers, FF&E coordinators, developers, and buying offices are welcome. Tell us your role and whether you need NDA or white-label packing — sales will align process and branding expectations.

Is this an online shop with inventory?

No. XZH Furniture uses this site for B2B project inquiries. There is no cart or live stock list here — programs are scoped and quoted by people.
